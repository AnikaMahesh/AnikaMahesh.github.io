About

Building robots that can understand the world around them.

I’m an electrical and computer engineering student and a research intern at MIT CSAIL, where I use vision-language models to track fish and guide a biomimetic turtle robot. Before that, I built underwater object detection pipelines at WHOI and wrote a sole-author paper on multi-robot communication for an ICRA 2026 workshop.

The ocean keeps me honest. Haze, light attenuation, and spotty connectivity break most of what works on land, so I focus on the part demos skip: knowing when a model is wrong. That has taken me into failure-mode analysis and interpretability probes for vision-language models.

Always happy to talk about perception, reliability, and robots that leave the lab.
